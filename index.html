Why split the work this way?
  • On-device (Gemini Nano): hooks and captions are short, private, and latency-sensitive. Running them locally is free, instant, and works offline — and the creator's ideas never leave the device.
  • In cloud (Gemini): a full 5-scene script needs long-form structure and reasoning, which needs a larger model.
  • Fallback: if Nano isn't available on a browser, the same prompts fall back to the cloud automatically — the app works for 100% of visitors.

How the hybrid works

◆

1 · On-device first

Hooks and captions use PREFER_ON_DEVICE. When Chrome's Gemini Nano is available, the draft never leaves the device — private, free, and fast enough to feel instant.

⬢

2 · Cloud for depth

The full script and titles use ONLY_IN_CLOUD with a larger Gemini model, because long-form structure needs more reasoning than a small on-device model can offer.

⟡

3 · Honest routing

Every response reports its real inferenceSource. The routing panel shows where each task ran, how long it took, and why — no marketing, just the telemetry.
